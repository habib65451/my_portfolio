About Lehdufe Habib

Electrical and electronics engineer focused on solar systems, fault analysis and consulting.

Background

Four years, one focus: power that works.

I'm an electrical and electronics engineer with four years of hands-on experience across solar PV design and installation, fault analysis, and electrical design engineering. I hold a B.Eng in Electrical and Electronics Engineering and have worked with residential, commercial and industrial clients across cameroon.

My approach starts with a proper site survey and load study, moves through design and documentation, and continues on-site through installation, testing and commissioning. I also work as an independent technical consultant, reviewing designs and advising on feasibility and energy efficiency for clients and project teams.

Outside of formal design work, I care about safety-first practice, clear technical communication with clients who may not have an engineering background, and systems that are built to be maintained, not just switched on.
